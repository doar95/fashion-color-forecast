Can machine learning forecast the Pantone Colour of the Year?

¿Puede el machine learning pronosticar el Color del Año? A database of every Pantone Colour of the Year since 2000, paint-industry colours and a century of decade palettes, 17 forecasting models tested year by year, and a pre-registered forecast for 2027.

Key findings / Hallazgos

  • Pantone looks for novelty: each colour moves further from the previous one than chance would predict (p ≈ 0.02). · Cada color se aleja del anterior más que por azar.
  • Machine learning does not beat the historical mean: the best of 17 models cut colour error by ~28% vs. a random pick, tied with the simple average. · El ML no le gana a la media histórica.
  • What works: combining base rates, the novelty rule and paint brands' early announcements put the true family in the top 3 in 12 of 16 years (75%). · La fusión de evidencia acertó la familia en el top 3 en 12 de 16 años.
